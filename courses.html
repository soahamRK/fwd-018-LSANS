Associated Department Courses

Courses offered by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering related to LSANS research domains.

Core Course • B.Tech / M.Tech

CSE-101: Introduction to Computer Programming

Fundamentals of programming logic, loops, arrays, and problem-solving methodologies in C and Python.

Advanced Elective • M.Tech / Ph.D.

CSE-302: Social Network Analysis & Graph Theory

Study of network topology, centrality measures, structural balance, link prediction, and influence maximization algorithms.

Specialized Seminar • Graduate Level

CSE-512: Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

Advanced cryptographic and differential privacy techniques for securing data and models in collaborative network analytics.
